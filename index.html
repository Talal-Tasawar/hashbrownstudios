The concept

Built for
sitting still.

Backyard started as exactly what its name says — a backyard. Wooden chairs under open sky, string lights strung tree to tree, and chai poured the way it should be: strong, hot, and completely unhurried.

No rush, no last call. Just a good seat, a warm cup, and a place in F-10 that doesn't ask you to leave until you're ready to.

When the big games are on

The garden gets loud.

For major live screenings — FIFA, cricket tournaments — Backyard turns into F-10's loudest backyard. A big screen, a full garden, and a crowd that showed up for the same reason you did.
